    Hike settings & calibration

    No GPS survived in the files, so the map estimates each photo's mile from its date along a northbound hike between the dates below. Fix any photo by opening it and entering an exact mile, or add calibration anchors here (check-ins with dates that you mark as anchors) to bend the date→mile curve to the pace you actually hiked.

    About this site

    Built like Pic2Map — an EXIF photo-location viewer — but for your Appalachian Trail collection. Pic2Map reads GPS coordinates embedded in photos; these files were stripped of GPS on the way out of the phone (a OnePlus 12 shot everything, Mar 3 – Sep 30, 2024), so location here is reconstructed from timestamps and your FarOut-style check-ins. Trail line: Appalachian Trail Conservancy GPS survey centerline (via appalachian-trail-data), stitched locally. Map tiles: OpenStreetMap. Everything runs locally; your check-ins, captions and mile fixes live in this browser (export them with ↓ Export).
